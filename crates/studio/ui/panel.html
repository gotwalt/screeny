Panel

The firmware spreads each colour's sub-level remainder over sixteen panel refreshes, walked so the dither does not blink up close (card 248), so a held colour resolves 1008 duty steps and only sRGB 0-4 are black. Bit planes is the same panel without that - 64 levels, nothing under sRGB 21 - which is how the darks behave for anything that does not hold still for about 100 ms. Aligned dark keeps the dithered panel above level 16 and puts every darker shade exactly on a level, so held dark shades do not blink up close (card 188).
